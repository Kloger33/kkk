# Site photos

Drop photos here and they appear on the site automatically after the next deploy.
Use JPG or WebP, landscape or portrait, at least 1200 px on the long side, under 500 KB each.
Only use photos you took yourself or have permission to use (ask people in the photo).

## Places (home page, "Khmer for the places you'll go")
The section appears when all three exist:

| File name        | What to photograph                                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `market.jpg`     | A stall at a Phnom Penh market (Central Market, Russian Market) |
| `tuktuk.jpg`     | A tuk-tuk or PassApp ride on a city street                      |
| `lesson.jpg`     | A real tutor teaching online: laptop, smiling, natural light    |

## Tutors (cards, profile, booking)
Name the file after the tutor's id, e.g. `tutors/tutor-1.jpg`.
A friendly head-and-shoulders photo, plain background, face clearly visible.
Without a photo, the tutor's initials are shown.

## Optional
| `welcome.jpg` | Shown in the log in / sign up window. A tutor and learner, or a warm Phnom Penh scene. |
